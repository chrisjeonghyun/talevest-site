2. Information and purposes

Within the permissions granted, the Operator processes:

(a) Channel and video identifiers; titles, descriptions, tags, playlists and thumbnail information; visibility, scheduled publication time, upload and processing status, to administer approved publication actions and verify results.

(b) Comment identifiers, text, publicly displayed author information, timestamps and approved replies on videos of the administered channels, to administer comments and verify actions. Personal information within comments is limited to what is necessary for that purpose.

(c) Channel- or video-level views, watch time, average view duration, subscriber changes and traffic sources, to evaluate content performance. Information identifying individual viewers is not requested.

(d) OAuth access and refresh tokens, granted scopes and validity information, to enable authorized API access. Google Account passwords are not collected.

(e) Operator approvals, API outcomes, incident and verification records, and inquiry contact information and content, to prevent unintended publication, respond to incidents and process requests.

3. Retention

(a) API metadata, comments and other raw API records, including copies, shall be refreshed or deleted within 30 days of collection or refresh. Refreshing a current copy does not refresh an older retained snapshot.

(b) Authorized analytics and statistics relating to the Operator's channels may be retained for comparative analysis for no more than 13 months. Authorization and continued existence of the corresponding video shall be checked at least every 30 days. Data for which these conditions cannot be verified shall not remain in retention. Public statistics obtained without owner authorization are excluded from this extended retention period.

(c) OAuth tokens shall be retained only while needed for authorized functions; use shall cease upon revocation.

(d) Following a deletion request or revocation through the Client, the associated stored data shall be deleted promptly and within seven days. Revocation through Google Account settings shall be detected through periodic checks and followed by prompt deletion, no later than 30 days after revocation.

(e) Inquiry content shall be deleted within 30 days after closure. A deletion record containing only the processing date, item count and outcome, excluding original content, tokens and reidentifying channel or video identifiers, may be retained for one year. Any legally required separate retention shall be identified by its legal basis and scope.

(f) These limits cover files, ledgers, logs, copies, backups and processor copies. Independently created scripts and original videos are distinguished from API-derived data; any API data embedded in them remains subject to this Policy.

4. Disclosure and supporting services

The Operator does not sell the information or disclose it for third parties' independent advertising, data-brokerage or other independent purposes. Materials transmitted to YouTube under approved publication actions, and videos or comments designated public, are processed according to those actions and visibility choices.

Operations use Google/YouTube, Operator-managed devices and an OpenClaw environment. Telegram and the AI services identified below may participate in approvals and reporting. Accordingly, “no third-party provision” does not mean that no external service processes information. Only information necessary for authorized operations shall be supplied to each service. Tokens and passwords shall not be included in reports, demonstrations or model inputs.

5. Access, deletion and revocation

Requests for access, correction, deletion, restriction or consent revocation may be sent to admin@studiopoly.ai, identifying the channel and requested scope. Do not send passwords or tokens. The Operator shall verify authority using only necessary information.

Users may also revoke access through the Google security permissions page or third-party connections: Google Account → connections to third-party apps and services → the relevant Client → remove access. Google-side revocation and deletion of locally held records are separate steps; the Operator remains responsible for the deletion periods in Section 3. Local deletion does not delete videos or comments already on YouTube.
